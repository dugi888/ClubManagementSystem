# Idea and plans



### In general

All classic club and player information by default:
- Available for anyone:
    - Names, positions, nationalities, birth dates, etc.
    - Club info
    - Club activities (news)
    - Track of results per club category

- Available for platform users divided in roles (admin, staff, players...):
    - Practices management system - coach is going to provide plan for the next week... (can add tactics simulations...)
    - Players are going to check if they are coming to the training and reason why not if..
    - Video analysis of the last match can be posted by the coach and players can comment on it
    - *Short quiz after the video analysis to check if players understood the key points
