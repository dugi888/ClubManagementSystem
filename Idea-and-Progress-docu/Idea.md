# Idea and plans

First idea was to create a classic CRUD app for players, staff, games etc. But from real life experience (as I am a player myself), some ideas came to my mind.
I want to create interactive system which will provide all needed (classic) information to fans and other, yet in depth it will have a lot more functionalities.
For staff, coaches and players it would be centralized system for game preparations, injury management, training management, video analysis and more.

Some use cases:
- Player is not able to come to training, he can inform coach and staff about it and reason why not.
- Player is injured and needs to inform staff about it, so they can manage his recovery and provide him with the best treatment.
- Training schedules, plans etc. Makes easier for all to organize and plan the next week.
- Coaches can post video analysis of the last match and players can comment on it. After that, coach can create a short quiz to check if players understood the key points.
- Streaming of matches (longshot).
- News Feed with articles, interviews, etc. 

I hope you get the idea, maybe it is not perfectly clear, but it is because I also don't know exactly how it will look like in the end. I have some ideas, but I want to see how it will evolve and what new ideas will come to my mind during the development :)
